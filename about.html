About

A little about me

I came to UX research through developmental psychology, spending three years at UMass Amherst's FAM Lab conducting mixed-methods research on stress and wellbeing in Latino adolescents, which included everything from behavioral studies to biological data collection. It was rigorous, meaningful work, and it gave me a strong foundation in how to ask good questions and what to do with the answers.

Over time, I found myself drawn to applying that foundation in a different context, one where the findings feed directly into product decisions. That's what brought me to JPMorgan Chase, where I now research the humans navigating some of the most complex internal systems in financial services.

When I'm off the clock, I'm usually cooking or baking, at a dance class, or on a soccer field. If not there, then watching TV with my dog Leia and cats Buckey and Earl.
