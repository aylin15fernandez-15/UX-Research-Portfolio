How I work

My process

The best research starts before the study starting with asking the right question, getting the right people involved, and making sure what I find actually lands with the people who need to act on it.

01
Get aligned

What is the Jobs To Be Done? What information do we already know, and what is the gap? I aim to identify this before a single session is scheduled.

02
Customize the research plan

Pick the method that aligns most effectively with the research objective, and design a systematic research guide that ensures the method is executed with complete objectivity and rigor.

03
Collect the data

Execute the study as designed while keeping the necessary stakeholders informed.

04
Make it move

Deliver key findings to that reflects the voice of the user and highlight what my stakeholders can do to improve the experience.
